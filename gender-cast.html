The cast

99Women and girls

185Men and boys

20Gender unspecified

This working census covers all 34 narrative sections. Recurring figures and transformed humans count once. The totals include explicitly numbered groups where the text gives a headcount, such as the forty princesses in Agib’s account. Indefinite crowds, spirits and talking animals are outside the totals. Sidi-Nouman’s wife is retained as a human-presenting character with ambiguous supernatural status.

With the forty princesses counted as one collective role, the women-and-girls total falls from 99 to 60. The group includes one woman who speaks for the others. The seven enslaved guards in the Baghdad tale remain in the unspecified column because the passage gives their number and task without explicitly identifying their gender.
