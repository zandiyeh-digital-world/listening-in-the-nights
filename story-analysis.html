    Method and sources

    Husain Haddawy, translator, The Arabian Nights, New Deluxe Edition, W. W. Norton, 2008, based on Muhsin Mahdi’s edition. References use PDF viewer page numbers. Open the consulted edition

    The relationship graph uses the 51 selected scene actions. The verb charts and word maps use a wider set of context-reviewed records from a scan of the complete text ranges, including embedded tales. The scan looked for selected verb forms near explicit subjects. Review prioritized identifiable names and stable roles, including the framing dialogue between Shahrazad and Dinarzad. Selected pronoun and narrative cases were added where context established the actor. The downloadable audit records each scan candidate as retained or excluded. Some labels summarize actions. The audit review is complete for this scan. Cases with unresolved subjects, duplicate references, or unsuitable wording were excluded. The fixed list and subject patterns leave other verbs outside the census. Nonhuman actors have their own group.

    The adjective bars group the existing fixed-list matches by descriptor type. Adjective groups follow matched references, and action groups follow coded actors. Compare counts within each measure. The graph shows relationships supported by the selected scenes and does not claim to list the full cast.
